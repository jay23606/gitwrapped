# Code of Conduct

We want gitwrapped to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience level, background, or identity.

**Do**
- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before judging.

**Don't**
- Harass, insult, or demean anyone.
- Post other people's private information.
- Spam or derail discussions.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may ban people who violate these rules.
To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository or contact a maintainer directly.
